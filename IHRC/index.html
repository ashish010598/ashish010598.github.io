ABOUT IHRC

Empowering Justice, Protecting Rights

The Indian Human Rights Commission (IHRC) safeguards and promotes the fundamental rights and dignity of every individual across India, guided by the principles of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

Through advocacy, education, and partnerships with diverse stakeholders, IHRC works to uphold equality, social justice, and the rule of law — with a special focus on protecting marginalised and vulnerable communities.
