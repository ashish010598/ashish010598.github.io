OUR MISSION

Built on a Foundation of Justice & Equality

At the Indian Human Rights Commission (IHRC), our mission is to safeguard and promote the fundamental rights and dignity of every individual across India. Guided by the principles of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, we are dedicated to combating discrimination, injustice, and violence in all forms.

Through advocacy, education, and partnerships with diverse stakeholders, IHRC works to uphold equality, social justice, and the rule of law, with a special focus on protecting the rights of marginalised and vulnerable communities.

Together, we aim to build a just and inclusive society where every individual can thrive with dignity and equality.

OUR CORE VALUES

Principles that Guide Us

Dignity & Equality

Every individual deserves respect and equal treatment, regardless of background, identity, or circumstances.

Justice & Fairness

We are committed to social justice and the rule of law, advocating fairness and accountability across society.

Inclusivity & Empowerment

We prioritise the voices of marginalised and vulnerable communities, working to empower them and promote their rights.

Integrity & Transparency

We uphold the highest standards of accountability and openness, fostering trust in all we do.
