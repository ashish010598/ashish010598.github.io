Our Work

What We Do

Defending dignity, advancing justice, and protecting fundamental rights across India.

Human Rights For Every Citizen
OUR FOCUS

Protecting Rights, Restoring Dignity

Human rights are moral principles or norms for certain standards of human behaviour and are regularly protected in municipal and international law. At IHRC, we work across communities, courts, and policy spaces to make these rights real for every citizen of India.

Our programmes address grievances at the grassroots, drive systemic reform, and equip citizens with the knowledge they need to defend their own rights and the rights of others.

HOW WE WORK

A Four-Pillar Approach

Advocacy & Legal Intervention

Filing petitions, supporting victims of rights violations, and engaging with agencies to influence policy and legislation.

Monitoring & Investigation

In-depth investigations into allegations of human rights abuses, particularly in cases involving marginalised communities.

Awareness & Education

Workshops, campaigns, and resources that empower citizens to know and exercise their fundamental rights.

Collaboration with Authorities

Working with civil society organisations, NGOs, and government bodies for a unified approach to rights protection.

OUR PROCESS

How We Take Action

A clear, accountable pathway from complaint to resolution.

  1. 01

    Submit Complaint

    Reach out via our complaint form, email or helpline with details of the rights violation.

  2. 02

    Review & Verify

    Our team reviews the case and verifies facts with the complainant, witnesses and local authorities.

  3. 03

    Investigate & Engage

    We conduct an in-depth investigation, engage relevant agencies, and provide legal support where required.

  4. 04

    Resolution & Follow-up

    We pursue resolution, monitor implementation, and follow up to ensure the complainant gets justice.
